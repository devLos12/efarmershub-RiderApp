# eFarmersHub Rider App — User Guide

**Version:** 1.0.0 | **Platform:** iOS & Android | **Last Updated:** April 2026

---

## 1. Login & Account Setup

### Login

![Screenshot]()

1. Open the eFarmersHub Rider App.
2. Enter your **Email Address**.
3. Enter your **Password**.
4. Tap **Sign In**.

### Forgot Password

![Screenshot]()

1. Tap **Forgot Password?** on the login screen.
2. Enter your email and tap **Send Code**.
3. Check your email for the 6-digit code.
4. Enter the code in the app.
5. Set your new password and tap **Change Password**.

### Set Availability Status

![Screenshot]()

1. Tap the **Profile** tab.
2. Toggle **Online Status** to **Available** to receive orders.
3. Toggle to **Offline** when not working.

### Update Profile

![Screenshot]()

1. Tap the **Profile** tab.
2. Tap **Edit Profile**.
3. Update your name, phone number, profile picture, wallet type, or wallet number.
4. Tap **Save Changes**.

---

## 2. Home Screen — Orders List

![Screenshot]()

Each order card shows the **Order ID**, **Customer Name**, **Delivery Address**, **Total Price**, and **Item Count**.

### View Order Details
1. Tap any order card to open the full details.

### Delete Orders
1. Tap **Select**.
2. Check the orders you want to remove.
3. Tap **Delete Selected** and confirm.

### Refresh
1. Pull down on the screen and release to refresh.

---

## 3. Order Details & Status Updates

![Screenshot]()

### Update Delivery Status
1. Tap an order from the Home screen.
2. Tap **Change Status** and select: **Pending**, **In Transit**, or **Delivered**.
3. Add a note (optional) — e.g. *"Left at door"*.
4. Tap **Capture Photo** (optional) for proof of delivery.
5. If payment is **Pending**, tap **Pick Receipt** and select from gallery.
6. Tap **Submit**.

---

## 4. Track Order Progress

![Screenshot]()

1. Open an order and tap **Track Order**.
2. View the status timeline with date, time, notes, and delivery photos.
3. Tap any photo to view full size.

---

## 5. Profile Management

![Screenshot]()

- **Edit Profile** — Update personal info and wallet details.
- **Online Status** — Toggle availability on or off.
- **Logout** — Tap **Logout**, confirm, and you'll return to the login screen.

---

## 6. Messages & Inbox

### Inbox

![Screenshot]()

1. Tap any conversation to open the chat.
2. Pull down to refresh and update unread counts.

### Sending a Message

![Screenshot]()

1. Type your message in the text field.
2. Tap **+** to attach images from your gallery.
3. Tap **Send**.

> **Note:** *Buyer* = customer chat. *Help & Support* = support team chat.

---

## 7. Payouts & Earnings

![Screenshot]()

1. Tap a payout card to view full details.
2. Tap the receipt image to view it full size.
3. Pull down to refresh the payouts list.

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Pending | Transfer in progress |
| Paid | Successfully sent to your wallet |

---

## 8. Payment Methods (QR Codes)

![Screenshot]()

1. Tap a QR code card to view it full screen.
2. Have the customer scan the QR to pay you.
3. Tap **X** to close.

> Make sure your wallet info is updated in Profile.

## 9. Other Features

### Location Tracking

![Screenshot]()

- Shows your current GPS coordinates (Latitude, Longitude, Accuracy).

### Camera — Proof of Delivery

![Screenshot]()

1. Tap **Capture Photo** when updating order status.

---

## Quick Tips

- Always set status to **Available** before expecting orders.
- Update order status regularly so customers are informed.
- Take delivery photos to avoid disputes.
- Keep your wallet number updated for correct payouts.
- Pull to refresh on any screen for the latest info.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|---------|
| Can't login | Check email/password. Use Forgot Password if needed. |
| Not receiving orders | Set status to Available and pull to refresh. |
| Messages not sending | Check internet connection and try again. |
| Payment not showing | Payouts may take 24–48 hours after delivery. |
| Camera not working | Enable camera permission in phone Settings. |
